About Water Us

Water Us is a small practice project for learning GitHub. It is also a handy way to remember to water your plants.

How a plant becomes thirsty

Every plant has a watering interval. Once it has gone that many days without water, it is thirsty. Press Water now and the count starts again from today.

Where your progress is kept

The day you last watered each plant is saved in your own browser, so plants get thirsty as real days pass. Nothing is sent anywhere. If you clear your browser data, or press Reset progress on the Plants page, every plant goes back to where it started.

Water Us is a practice project for learning GitHub. Care notes are general guidance, not professional advice.
